Why for top-p = 0.8 SFT-LCD and SFT-gen had a worst score than SFT(-noLCD) and (noLCD-)gen? 
I think the dataset introduces more entropy in the distribution, so pass@1 is worse and pass@128 is better for SFT(-noLCD) and (noLCD-)gen. 
However, this greater entropy means that when masking, the mass get redistributed to the valid tokens and the top-p=0.8 prevents a diverse sampling. 


When we use top-p = 1 (for LCD-gen) on the LCD-SFT model, we see that the model behaves in the same way as the noLCD-SFT noLCD-gen (this one uses top_p=0.95 for some reason).

We hope that LCD-SFT learns/generalizes faster (why?) but to see this we need a better task.

(If masking some tokens allow to generalize better, why don't we mask the 90% tokens the less probable each time?)
